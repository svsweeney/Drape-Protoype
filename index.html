Size guide
How to measure
1
Chest / bust — around the fullest part of your chest, tape parallel to the floor. Leave a finger's width of room.
2
Waist — around your natural waist, roughly an inch above your belly button. Breathe out naturally first.
3
Hips — around the fullest part of your hips, 7–9 inches below your waist. Stand with feet together.
4
Inside leg — from your crotch seam to the bottom of your ankle. Stand straight, feet slightly apart.
